About Khula-Nathi

"Grow with Us"

Empowering underserved communities through sustainable food, gardens, and community support.

Our Mission & Vision

Our mission is to empower underserved communities through education and sustainable development initiatives. We envision a future where every individual has equal access to opportunities for growth and prosperity.

What We Do

Khula-Nathi grows and shares fresh, healthy food with families who cannot afford enough to eat and supports local schools in feeding their learners, building a stronger, more food-secure community through gardening, donations, and volunteer efforts.

GROWING TOGETHER

Helping families • Supporting schools • Building stronger communities
