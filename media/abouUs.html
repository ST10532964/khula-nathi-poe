Our Mission and Vision

Our mission is to empower underserved communities through education and sustainable development initiatives. We envision a future where every individual has equal access to opportunities for growth and prosperity

What We Do:

Khula-Nathi grows and shares fresh, healthy food with families who cannot afford enough to eat and supports local schools in feeding their learners, building a stronger ,more food secure community through gardening ,donation and volunteer effort
